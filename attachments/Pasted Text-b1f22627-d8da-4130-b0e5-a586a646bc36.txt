SOUR PAINT STUDIO CANVAS — MASTER BRIEF (FINAL)
Consolidated: base model + Gemini + Meta + GPT musical gesture layer + Claude addenda,
with Grok corrections applied.
For the build chat: this is the spec. Add only. Do not replace the fluid model with a
note-to-color chart. Do not reopen rejected items in section 16.

================================================================================
1. THE APP (LOCKED)
================================================================================
Name: Sour Paint Studio Canvas
Platform: phone, portrait, iOS/Android.
The player plays guitar. Singing is allowed. The phone mic listens. The app paints
in real time. The painting is also the video of that performance. The player watches
the picture form while they play.
No menu. No brush picker. No color picker. Music is the only input.

================================================================================
2. THE PROBLEM (LOCKED)
================================================================================
Literal lookup rules ("if it hears G, use this color") produce mechanical, dead output.
The fix is not better rules. The fix is physics. The canvas has its own life.
The music nudges it. It does not draw what it hears.
A visualizer (one visual event per detected note) is a failure.

================================================================================
3. CORE MODEL (LOCKED)
================================================================================
Canvas is a fluid simulation. Paint is pigment in a medium. It flows, pools, bleeds,
and dries on its own. Music applies forces. The same riff twice never paints the same
thing, because pigment from the first pass is still moving when the second hits.

Music to behavior:
- Sustained note = slow current dragging pigment
- Fast run = turbulence
- Chord = splash that radiates
- Silence = drying: settle, soften, optional craze cracks after a long silence

Feature to force:
- Pitch class = which pigment family gets stirred. Never a fixed RGB.
- Octave = stroke weight. Low = thick and broad. High = thin and narrow.
- Amplitude = force magnitude and pressure
- Tempo = churn rate and advection speed
- Chord quality = viscosity and complement bias
- Key change = movement reset and new dominant hue, no hard color swap
- Mood = slow independent state. See section 8.

================================================================================
4. PIPELINE (LOCKED)
================================================================================
STAGE 1 — DETECT
Mic to AudioWorklet. Not ScriptProcessorNode.
Per frame: f0 (YIN or MPM), RMS amplitude, spectral centroid, chroma vector, onset.
Derive pitch class 0–11, octave normalized, chord quality, tempo.
5-frame median on f0. Reject pitch jumps greater than 12 semitones in under 100 ms.
50 ms exponential moving average on RMS before mapping.
Max RMS jump per frame: 0.30. Clamp harder jumps.
AGC artifact: if RMS jumps more than 0.5 in under 80 ms with no onset, discard that
frame's amplitude and hold the previous valid value.
This is the only place audio is understood.

STAGE 2 — INTERPRET
Detected values become forces. See section 6. Deterministic.

STAGE 3 — SIMULATE
Grid of velocity (x, y), pigment density, water, height.
Semi-Lagrangian advection. 1 Jacobi diffusion pass on the live path. 2 only if the
phone holds frame budget. Not 3. No full Navier-Stokes solver.
Fluid has memory.

STAGE 4 — RENDER
Density to color and opacity. Height to normal map. Raking light from upper left, 30 degrees.
Video is the frame sequence. Stages 3–4 are chaotic because of memory.

================================================================================
5. GEMINI ADDITIONS (LOCKED)
================================================================================
5.1 DYNAMIC IMPASTO
Height field is part of the simulation, not decoration.
High amplitude deposits body, not just color.
Hard strum = ridge. Sustained low note = plateau. Fast run = broken ridges.
Soft playing and silence deposit no new body. Ridges settle.
On screen: fake 3D via normal map. Paint stands up when the player digs in.
Height blocks flow. Ridges are barriers. This is canvas memory.

5.2 CHROMATIC DARK MIXING
Hard ban on pure black #000000 and flat achromatic dark.
Darks only from unequal complementary pairs: blue/orange, red/green, yellow/violet.
Never 50/50. Dark must stay biased: blue-black, orange-brown, still readable as the hue.
Complement taken from pigment already nearby, not a fixed lookup.
Shadow under a ridge is a complementary mix of that stroke.
Complement ratio clamp 0.42 max. Above that is mud.

================================================================================
6. PARAMETER MAPPING TABLE (LOCKED)
================================================================================
SESSION
3-second mic calibration, then normalize. Guitar range clamped E2–E6.
No hard musical thresholds.

PER-FRAME INPUTS
f0 = fundamental Hz
pc = pitch class 0–11
oct = 0.0–1.0 across E2–E6
amp_norm = RMS / (rolling 95th percentile + epsilon), slow AGC
centroid = spectral brightness 0–1
chord_val = {major: 0.0, ambiguous: 0.3, dom7: 0.5, minor: 1.0} * chord_confidence
onset = transient strength 0–1
churn = tempo mapped 40 bpm to 0.1, 200 bpm to 2.0, clamped 0.1–2.0
silence = amp_norm < 0.08 for more than 300 ms
voice_active = see section 9

MAPPING
amp_norm to force = amp_norm * (0.4 + 0.6 * onset). Do not use an unbounded 0–2.5 curve.
amp_norm to height deposit only if amp_norm > 0.45. Rate = (amp_norm - 0.45) * 1.8 per frame, along velocity. Below 0.45, height does not grow. Color still deposits.
amp_norm to ridge width 2–20 px, linear, sim pixels.
oct to kernel width = 14 - 13 * oct, clamp 1–14 sim px. Low = wide. High = narrow.
f0 to stroke weight, log scale, clamp 1–12 sim px. Low = heavy.
pc to hue family, 30 degree steps. Jitter from mood, cap plus or minus 12 degrees. Not plus or minus 20.
centroid to saturation 0.30–0.90, linear, clamped. Does not override pitch class. May nudge temperature toward the warmer or cooler neighbor.
chord_val to viscosity: 0 thin, 1 thick. major 0, ambiguous and dom7 about 0.5, minor 1. Low confidence falls back toward 0.3. Do not use the 0.70–1.30 scale.
chord_val to complement ratio = clamp(chord_val * 0.42, 0.05, 0.42). Major about 0.05–0.12. Minor 0.28–0.42. Never 0.50.
churn to advection speed multiplier, direct, 0.1–2.0.
onset > 0.55 to one splash. Radius = onset * 80 sim px, minimum 20. One per onset.
silence to drying rate ramp 0.01 to 0.20 per frame over 4.5 s. Snap back on any onset. No new pigment. No new height. Diffusion halved. Height edges blur.

COMPLEMENT FORMULA
complement_hue = (local_hue + 180) mod 360
complement_ratio = clamp(chord_val * 0.42, 0.05, 0.42)
dark_hue = lerp(local_hue, complement_hue, complement_ratio)
dark_L = target_L * 1.15
Never (0,0,0).

CLAMPS
Value floor 0.12. Under that, add complement, not black.
Saturation floor 0.18 on any dark. A gray dark is rejected and remixed with 10 percent more local hue.
Height cap 1.0. Above that, deposit smears sideways.
Luminance floor: if canvas average luminance < 0.08 for 3 seconds, inject 0.03 * family L into all cells. Only global brightness override.
AGC epsilon always on. First 3 seconds use a default 95th percentile.

DYNAMICS BANDS
amp_norm < 0.35: watercolor. Thin, transparent, fine, small. Height stays 0. Color still deposits.
0.35–0.65: oil/ink. More saturation, broader gesture, little body.
> 0.65: impasto, splash, large stroke. Uses the height rule.

================================================================================
7. GESTURE HOLDER (LOCKED) — BUILD THIS FIRST
================================================================================
Gestures are musical. Not taps, pinches, swipes, or phone shake. Touch sculpting is out.
Music is the only input.

One active gesture. A phrase is one stroke. Notes inside the phrase extend it.
No per-note dots.

STATE
active: bool
trail: array of {x, y, t, amp, f0, onset}
hue: degrees
family: 0–11
started_at: timestamp
last_onset: timestamp

RULES
- Single note = one small brush gesture
- Rapid picking = connected marks on the same gesture
- Slow bend (f0 glide) = active stroke curves
- Vibrato (f0 wobble, period under 200 ms, depth over 3 percent) = stroke wiggles
- Sustained note = extend the stroke. Do not start a new mark. Paint keeps flowing.
- Big strum (onset high, amp high, chord) = one large movement
- Chord held = no new stroke. Existing paint bleeds, blends, and expands for the hold.
- Silence = nothing new. Drying only.
Phrase end = silence over 300 ms, or a new onset after a hold. Commit the trail. Next note may open a new gesture.

TRAIL TO FORCE
Each trail sample is a force, not a dot.
Position is territory, not a Y coordinate. Low octaves bias the lower third. High octaves bias the upper third. Mid sits center.
Direction follows the velocity field. The trail seeds where the stroke starts. The fluid carries it.
Magnitude uses the locked force formula.
Color deposits along the trail. Height deposits only under the height rule.

COMMIT
On phrase end the trail becomes a permanent pigment deposit. The fluid keeps advecting it.

TEST
Play E–G–C–G–E as one phrase. Expect one woven stroke across three territories, not five dots.
Play it again. Different result, because the fluid has memory.

================================================================================
8. MOOD (LOCKED)
================================================================================
mood = (warmth, energy), both 0.05–0.95. Start (0.5, 0.5).
warmth 0 = cool, 1 = warm.
energy 0 = slow, wet, saturated. 1 = fast, broken, lighter.

Modulates:
Hue tilt capped at plus or minus 12 degrees, inside the pitch-class band.
Drying: high energy adds 0.04 per frame.
Advection: energy * 0.8 added to churn.
Impasto settle: low energy height * 0.999 per frame. High energy height * 0.997.
Saturation: warmth 0 pulls floor toward 0.4. Warmth 1 lifts ceiling toward 0.95.

Update per frame:
target_warmth = minor 0.2, major 0.8, dom7 0.6, ambiguous 0.5, plus centroid * 0.1
target_energy = churn / 2
decay = 0.994
mood = lerp(mood, target, 1 - decay)
Add gaussian noise 0.008 per axis. Clamp 0.05–0.95.
Key change: lerp 40 percent toward (0.5, 0.5), then resume inertia.

================================================================================
9. VOCAL LAYER (LOCKED)
================================================================================
Detect voice with HNR over 15 dB, formant cluster 500–3000 Hz, sustain envelope vs guitar decay.
voice_active boolean, 80 ms hysteresis. No ML.
Voice never deposits height.
Voice never sets force direction. No curl. Guitar owns the velocity field.
Voice: kernel width * 2.5, splash suppressed except consonant micro-splash at 0.3 radius,
optional formant hue shift plus or minus 8 degrees inside the current family.
Simultaneous: guitar drives velocity. Voice wash at 40 percent alpha, saturation cap 0.55, same family.
If both have pitch, the louder source wins the family. Do not average them.

================================================================================
10. SESSION PALETTE (LOCKED)
================================================================================
Ground: first 30 seconds, modal pitch class and chord quality. After calibration, deposit once at 0.12 alpha as a soft bloom. Never recalculate. No warm/cool picker.

Key-relative spacing, not a hex chart. Root hue drifts with mood.
fifth +60, fourth +45, major third +30, minor third -30, tritone +150, others interpolate.
If key confidence is low, skip this and use pitch-class families only.

Palette memory: last 120 seconds of deposited hue centers. Bias current stroke 15 percent toward the most-used. Last 30 seconds weighted 3x.

Key method: Krumhansl-style correlation on a 30 second chroma histogram. Confidence = best minus second best. If confidence < 0.15, skip key-relative mapping. Open tunings fail safe. Root may update after 30 seconds if confidence rises; relationships lerp over 3 seconds. Ground does not recalculate.

Tempo method: median inter-onset interval of the last 8 onsets. Ignore gaps over 2 seconds. Churn smoothed over 300 ms. Rubato does not retarget every late note.

================================================================================
11. PHONE REAL-TIME (LOCKED)
================================================================================
Sim grid 270x480 float16. Render 1080x1920. Not 256x256. High end may use 360x640. Floor 180x320. Never simulate at 1080x1920.
Upscale bicubic. Build normals at sim res, bicubic the normal map, then 1 px unsharp on normals only, amount 0.35. Do not sharpen color. Raking light after that.
Textures: velocity RG16f, pigment RGBA (RGB density, A water), height R16f.
Audio thread: 512 samples at 48 kHz. Pitch on audio thread. Ring buffer to render thread.
Budget: if 60 fps cannot hold, run live at 30 fps. Audio still analyzed every hop.
Allocate buffers at init. Nothing allocated inside the frame loop.
Pre-warm 12 pigment families into a 3D LUT. Sample the LUT.

================================================================================
12. FAILURE MODES (LOCKED)
================================================================================
Mud: per-pixel mix_count. If more than 6 mixes in under 5 seconds, force complement_ratio 0.02 and inject clean water. Also saturation decay: pigment older than 8 seconds loses 0.015 saturation per frame, floor 0.30. Hue unchanged.
Black holes: if luminance under 0.15, dark = lerp(dark, base * 1.3, 0.5).
Strobe: global force budget per second. Scale new forces when over.
Empty canvas on fingerpicking: always deposit color even if height is 0. Minimum flow Perlin * 0.2 at low amp.
Latency: onset and amp drive force immediately. Pitch and chord may lag 100 ms.
Mic picks speaker: vocal band split. 80–800 Hz guitar driver. 800–4000 Hz voice texture.
Stuck silence pools: gravity drift 0.008 per frame downward during silence only. Clears on onset.
Flat soft passage: if luminance stddev under 0.05 for 6 seconds, next onset force times 1.8, once.
Bad octave: 5-frame median, reject 12-semitone jumps, hold last valid pitch class.

================================================================================
13. EXTRA PAINTERLY BEHAVIORS (LOCKED)
================================================================================
Edge bloom / backrun: water > 0.6 and velocity < 0.1 and divergence < -0.2. Spawn ring, center density * 0.9, push outward 2x. Loud to soft.
Granulation: tag ultramarine, burnt sienna, cerulean, raw umber. If viscosity > 0.08 and velocity < 0.2, multiply density by paper bump. Also, velocity under 0.08: 4 percent chance per cell to move 15 percent density to densest neighbor.
Sustained-pitch bloom: one pitch class over 60 percent of readings for 2 seconds. Radial wash alpha 0.12. No height.
Edge bleed: 0.018 density per frame along density gradient. If height gradient > 0.3, bleed drops to 0.004. Ridges stay hard.
Ghost layer: every 30 seconds of active play, composite pigment at 5 percent under new paint. Export toggle default off.
Craze: only after 10 seconds of silence, on height > 0.4, chromatic dark at complement 0.35–0.40, 1–2 px. Not at 1.5 seconds.
Late soft smear: if amp_norm < 0.15 after a peak over 0.7 in the last 8 seconds, and average height > 0.25, smear existing height at 30 percent advection. No new body.
Key change: chroma histogram over 8 seconds. Correlation under 0.6 triggers a 3 second hue lerp, a curl-force sweep, and a 20 percent water dump. Not a hard cut.

================================================================================
14. EXPORT (LOCKED)
================================================================================
Master: store sim params + seed + audio. Re-render offline, up to 4K. Do not store every sim frame as the master.
Quick share: MediaRecorder of the display canvas only, 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 or H.265, about 8 Mbps, drop to 5 on memory pressure. Audio is the mic at -3 dB, optional light reverb, or muted.
Still: highest luminance-variance frame by default. Scrub allowed. No filters.
Sidecar JSON: key or null, duration, peak force time and value, peak impasto time and height, silence fraction, mood warmth and energy every 10 seconds, app version.
Review screen: still, scrub, Export, Share. No editing tools.
No multi-session layering in v1. A previous frame would need a restored height field. Out of scope.
